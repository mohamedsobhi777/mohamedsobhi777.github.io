I am an MPhil researcher in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working with Dr. Qifeng Chen's Visual Intelligence Group. My research interests are embodied AI, humanoid robotics, robot learning, and vision-language-action models.

At the HKUST Robotics Institute, I work on turning monocular human videos into whole-body humanoid control policies. My work connects human motion recovery, motion retargeting, generative modeling, and robot deployment.

## Research interests

- **Humanoid robotics:** whole-body control, motion retargeting, and teleoperation.
- **Robot learning:** imitation learning and generative policies.
- **Embodied AI:** vision-language-action models and real-world evaluation.

## Selected work

[Drone Fly](https://github.com/mohamedsobhi777/drone-fly) explores connectome-based drone navigation in simulation. [LLM Reasoning with GRPO](https://github.com/mohamedsobhi777/countdown-grpo) studies reinforcement learning for language-model reasoning. See the [projects page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for more.

## Background

I earned a BEng in Computer Science and AI from HKUST in 2025. Before my current research, I worked on machine learning at Upsurge Labs, time-series forecasting at Ansel Intelligence, and steerable language-model decoding at ETH Zürich's Secure, Reliable, and Intelligent Systems Lab.
