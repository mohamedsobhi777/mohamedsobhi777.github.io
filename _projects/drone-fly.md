I built a camera-guided MuJoCo quadcopter simulator using an 80-neuron fruit-fly connectome and a 339-parameter imitation-trained readout. The system completed 12 of 12 held-out courses, matching baseline controllers.

The accompanying React and Three.js flight lab exposes live camera and neural telemetry, causal interventions, replay, and background experiments. I evaluated controllers and ablations across 96 simulated flights.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/mohamedsobhi777/drone-fly).
